# Supporting ReddCoin Users

Most ReddCoin questions come from users new to running a node, setting
up a wallet, or staking for the first time. Anyone with practical
experience can answer them. Volunteer support is one of the highest
leverage things you can contribute, because it scales the project
beyond what the core maintainers can handle directly.

Before answering, it's worth bookmarking:

- The [Operator Guides](/guides) on this site for how-to walkthroughs.
- The [Glossary](/glossary) for terminology questions.
- The [Protocol Developer Guide](/protocol/devguide) for "how does
  X work under the hood" questions.

## Community channels

ReddCoin coordinates support across a few channels. Hop into whichever
you're most comfortable with; cross-posting between them when an
answer is broadly useful is welcome.

- **Discord** — the project's primary live-chat channel. Look for the
  invite link on [reddcoin.com](https://reddcoin.com) or in the
  [`reddcoin-project/reddcoin`](https://github.com/reddcoin-project/reddcoin)
  README.

- **Telegram** — secondary live-chat channel, often more active in
  some regions and time zones.

- **Reddit** —
  [/r/reddCoin](https://www.reddit.com/r/reddCoin/) is the main
  ReddCoin subreddit and a good place for longer-form questions and
  announcements.

- **X / Twitter** — the project posts updates and amplifies community
  questions; tagging the official account in a question often surfaces
  someone with an answer.

- **GitHub Discussions** at
  [`reddcoin-project/reddcoin`](https://github.com/reddcoin-project/reddcoin/discussions)
  for technical questions that benefit from being written down and
  searchable later.

## Tips for answering well

- **Link to the docs.** Most common questions ("how do I check my
  staking weight?", "where's my wallet file?", "what does this debug
  log entry mean?") have an answer somewhere in
  [docs.reddcoin.com](https://docs.reddcoin.com). Linking the doc
  *and* paraphrasing the relevant bit is more useful than either on
  its own.

- **Don't share credentials.** Never ask a user to share private keys,
  seed phrases, or wallet files; never share your own. Scammers
  occasionally try to extract these by impersonating support.

- **Escalate when needed.** For suspected security issues see the
  [Bug reports](issues#responsible-disclosure) page. For complex
  protocol or consensus questions, ping a Core maintainer in the
  appropriate channel rather than guessing.
